The Problem

Profit rarely disappears all at once. It leaks.

A customer stays on an old rate. A completed task never reaches an invoice. A supplier charge drifts above the agreed terms. A failed payment is never followed up.

Each issue can look too small to investigate on its own. Across many customers, contracts and transactions, those gaps can quietly reduce the profit the business keeps.

↳The question is not only how much you sell. It is whether the business is collecting and retaining what it has already earned.

FAQ

Good questions deserve straight answers.

Still unsure whether the audit is appropriate? The application is a first conversation, not a commitment to buy.

What is the Profit Leak Audit?
It is a focused review of where money may be leaking between what your business intended to charge or collect and what actually happened. Depending on scope, the work can cover revenue, billing, collections, contracts and recurring spend.
How is this different from an accountant or auditor?
Accounting and audit work is essential for accurate records and controls. Our focus is the operational and commercial gap between what should have been charged, collected or paid and what actually occurred. This service is not a financial statement audit or assurance opinion.
What do you need from us?
Depending on the agreed scope, we may request read only access to selected billing or accounting systems, relevant contracts, payment information and recurring spend records. We aim to request only information relevant to the review. Do not send passwords or private authentication credentials through this website.
How long does the audit take?
The target is around two weeks from kickoff to the findings session for a suitably scoped engagement. Timing depends on complexity, record quality and access. The expected timeline is confirmed before work begins.
Do you guarantee a savings amount?
No. A savings figure should come from evidence, not a promise. The audit is designed to identify and quantify substantiated findings within the agreed scope. Actual recovery depends on the circumstances and actions taken.
How does the recovery fee work?
Recovery is separate from the $2,000 audit. Where recovery work is agreed, any fixed implementation fee and performance component are documented in a separate written agreement. The agreed method defines what counts as verified recovered value or savings.
What happens to our data?
Information submitted through this site is used to respond to your request. For client work, we aim to limit information requests and access to what is needed for the agreed scope. Read the for more detail.
Can you show client results or references?
We do not publish invented testimonials or unverified savings claims. You can ask to walk through the audit method and an illustrative sample report format before deciding whether to proceed. Illustrations are not representations of client outcomes.
Is the audit right for every business?
Not necessarily. It is most useful where enough customers, invoices, contracts, transactions or recurring costs exist for gaps to hide inside normal activity. We will discuss fit before asking you to commit to an engagement.
