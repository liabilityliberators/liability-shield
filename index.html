================= PROBLEM / THE LEAK =================

The Problem

Revenue can disappear without ever looking like a crisis.

Leakage rarely announces itself. It hides inside ordinary financial processes between the systems that are all working correctly, and the money that never quite arrives.

Revenue flow illustrative system view

Ordinary on the surface

Invoices go out. Payments arrive. Reports reconcile. Nothing looks broken which is exactly why the gaps persist for years.

Distributed across systems

Contracts live in one system, billing in another, payments in a third. Every handoff is a place where value can quietly fall through.

Too small to notice, too large to ignore

Individually, most discrepancies look trivial. In aggregate, they can represent a meaningful share of revenue you already earned.

================= WHAT WE FIND =================

What We Find

Eight places revenue quietly goes missing.

Our analysis covers the full revenue cycle from contract terms to cash application looking for money that was earned, billed, or paid incorrectly, and never corrected.

01

Unpaid invoices

Overdue receivables that were never systematically worked, escalated, or resolved.

02

Underpayments

Customers paying less than invoiced short pays, partial remittances, and rounding gaps.

03

Billing discrepancies

Invoices that don't match contracted terms, order quantities, or agreed rates.

04

Contract leakage

Missed price escalations, unenforced terms, and entitlements nobody is tracking.

05

Missed charges

Work delivered, services rendered, or usage accumulated that never made it onto an invoice.

06

Unapplied cash

Payments received but never matched to open receivables, aging artificially on the books.

07

Credits & deductions

Duplicate credits, unresolved deductions, and write offs accepted without challenge.

08

Pricing discrepancies

Customers billed at outdated or incorrect prices across SKUs, tiers, and contracts.
