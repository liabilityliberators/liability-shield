FAQ

The questions that matter
before anything is connected.

Liability Liberators starts with measurement and operational context. We do not require a facility to jump straight into hardware or automation.

What exactly does Liability Liberators do?

We build an operating model of how a facility produces, purchases, stores and consumes electricity. The first commercial engagement is the Energy Autonomy Assessment. From there, customers can move into recurring optimization and, where the technical conditions support it, control integration.

Who is the Energy Autonomy Assessment for?

It is designed for industrial and infrastructure operators with meaningful electricity costs, complex power arrangements, multiple energy sources, or a clear need to improve operating visibility and resilience. One facility is enough to begin.

What do I need to provide?

We start with the information that already exists: utility bills, meter data where available, generation or storage information, major load information, and relevant operating context. The application helps determine what is actually available before work begins.

Do you install solar panels, batteries or generators?

The initial Liability Liberators product is the intelligence and control layer. Where deployment requires physical equipment or electrical work, those components are scoped separately and can involve qualified third party engineering, electrical and equipment partners.

Will the assessment guarantee a specific amount of savings?

No. The assessment identifies and quantifies opportunities supported by the available facility data. Any financial impact estimate is treated as an analysis to validate, not a guaranteed outcome.

What happens after the $5,000 assessment?

You receive the operating model, prioritized findings and a control path. Where recurring optimization is appropriate, the next step is the Continuous Optimization offering at $10,000 per month. Control and microgrid projects are scoped separately because their requirements depend on the facility.

Can you work with facilities outside Uganda?

Yes. The assessment can begin remotely where the required data can be shared electronically. Physical commissioning and electrical work depend on site location, applicable standards, local partners and project scope.

How does the interactive System View relate to the product?

The System View is a product architecture explorer. It shows how sources, storage, control logic and facility loads fit together. It is not presented as a live feed from a customer's facility unless that facility has actually been connected to the system.

When does automation become possible?

Automation depends on data quality, device access, control interfaces, safety requirements and the decisions the customer is comfortable delegating. We favor bounded automation with explicit operating limits and commissioning procedures.

How do you handle information submitted through the website?

Website applications are transmitted through the form delivery service configured for Liability Liberators and are used to review and respond to the request. See the Privacy Policy below for the fuller description of collection, processing, retention, rights and security practices.

Scope and who this policy covers
