The Problem

Profit rarely vanishes. It leaks.

A business can have strong sales and still leave meaningful money behind. The losses are usually scattered across small operational decisions that nobody owns.

One failed payment gets forgotten. A customer stays on an old price. A supplier bills above the agreed rate. A team keeps paying for software nobody needs. A completed piece of work never makes it onto an invoice.

Each leak feels too small to chase. Together, they change the profit you actually keep.

What We Find

We look where money slips.

Not a generic business review. We follow the trail from what should happen to what actually happened.

Revenue that walks away

Failed payments, dormant customers, missed renewals and opportunities that were never followed through.

Billing that misses the mark

Price drift, unbilled work, change orders, discounts that never reset and usage that never gets charged.

Contracts nobody enforces

Supplier overcharges, weak renewal controls, scope creep and terms that quietly cost more than they should.

Spend that keeps bleeding

Unused tools, duplicate vendors, excess recurring spend, inefficient purchasing and routine write offs.
