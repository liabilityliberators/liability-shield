FAQ

Questions founders actually ask.

"What exactly happens when I call the emergency line?"
A human answers within 3 rings. We immediately begin intake: what happened, where it's spreading, who the stakeholders are, and what you need. Within 15 minutes, you have a preliminary legal exposure assessment and 2-3 strategic response options. Within 30 minutes, we're executing the one you choose.
"Do you actually have lawyers, or do you just draft statements?"
We are not a law firm. We are crisis managers who work alongside your existing counsel — or connect you with vetted legal partners in our network if you don't have one. We handle the coordination, narrative strategy, and stakeholder communication so your lawyers can focus on the law.
"Can you guarantee a story will be killed?"
No ethical crisis manager can guarantee that. What we guarantee is speed, coordination, and strategic positioning. Sometimes the best move is to get ahead of the story. Sometimes it's to correct the record. Sometimes it's to let it burn out while protecting what matters: your investors, your customers, and your legal exposure.
"What's the difference between this and a PR agency?"
PR agencies are built for campaigns. We're built for emergencies. A traditional PR firm takes 24-48 hours to respond. We take 7 minutes. We also integrate legal assessment into every response — something PR firms typically don't touch. Think of us as your crisis SWAT team, not your marketing department.
"Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?"
Month-to-month. No annual contracts. If we're not delivering value, you shouldn't pay. That said, most clients who sign up during a crisis stay on afterward — because once you've seen how fast a controlled response works, you don't go back to hoping for the best.

Liability Liberators ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our crisis workbook, or engage our services.

We may collect information about you in a variety of ways, including:

  • Personal Data: Name, email address, phone number, company name, and job title that you voluntarily provide when requesting assessments, contacting us, or submitting the crisis workbook.
  • Crisis Workbook Data: Information you enter into our interactive workbook, including self-assessment scores, team roster details, scenario responses, and strategic planning notes.
  • Usage Data: Information our servers automatically collect when you access the site, such as your browser type, operating system, access times, and the pages you have viewed.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Comply with legal obligations and protect our rights
  • Send you marketing communications (only with your consent, which you may withdraw at any time)

3. Confidentiality & Security

Given the sensitive nature of crisis management, we take extraordinary measures to protect your data:

  • All workbook submissions are reviewed only by authorized crisis response personnel
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties

  • Access to client data is restricted on a need-to-know basis
  • We maintain strict confidentiality agreements with all team members and partners

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors who assist in operating our website and services (e.g., hosting providers, email services), bound by confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, property, or safety, or that of our clients or others.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, financing, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including to satisfy legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.
