FAQ

Questions founders actually ask.

"What exactly happens when I call the emergency line?"
A human answers within 3 rings. We immediately begin intake: what happened, where it's spreading, who the stakeholders are, and what you need. Within 15 minutes, you have a preliminary legal exposure assessment and 2-3 strategic response options. Within 30 minutes, we're executing the one you choose.
"Do you actually have lawyers, or do you just draft statements?"
We are not a law firm. We are crisis managers who work alongside your existing counsel — or connect you with vetted legal partners in our network if you don't have one. We handle the coordination, narrative strategy, and stakeholder communication so your lawyers can focus on the law.
"Can you guarantee a story will be killed?"
No ethical crisis manager can guarantee that. What we guarantee is speed, coordination, and strategic positioning. Sometimes the best move is to get ahead of the story. Sometimes it's to correct the record. Sometimes it's to let it burn out while protecting what matters: your investors, your customers, and your legal exposure.
"What's the difference between this and a PR agency?"
PR agencies are built for campaigns. We're built for emergencies. A traditional PR firm takes 24-48 hours to respond. We take 7 minutes. We also integrate legal assessment into every response — something PR firms typically don't touch. Think of us as your crisis SWAT team, not your marketing department.
"Is there a contract or can I cancel anytime?"
Month-to-month. No annual contracts. If we're not delivering value, you shouldn't pay. That said, most clients who sign up during a crisis stay on afterward — because once you've seen how fast a controlled response works, you don't go back to hoping for the best.
