FAQ

Questions fintech founders actually ask.

"What exactly happens when I call the emergency line?"
A human answers within 3 rings. We immediately begin intake: what happened, where it is spreading, which banking partners are involved, and what your S-1 timeline looks like. Within 15 minutes, you have a preliminary regulatory exposure assessment and 2-3 strategic response options. Within 30 minutes, we are executing the one you choose.
"Do you replace our regulatory counsel or S-1 lawyers?"
We are not a law firm. We are crisis managers who work alongside your existing counsel — or connect you with vetted fintech regulatory partners in our network if you do not have one. We handle the coordination, narrative strategy, and banking partner communication so your lawyers can focus on the filings and regulatory submissions.
"Can you guarantee a story will be killed?"
No ethical crisis manager can guarantee that. What we guarantee is speed, coordination, and strategic positioning. Sometimes the best move is to get ahead of the story. Sometimes it is to correct the record. Sometimes it is to let it burn out while protecting what matters: your S-1 timeline, your banking partnerships, and your regulatory standing.
"What is the difference between this and a fintech law firm?"
Big law firms are essential for S-1 legal work and regulatory filings. They are not built for 2 AM crisis deployment. We take 7 minutes. We also integrate banking partner relations and financial media strategy into every response — something law firms typically do not touch. Think of us as your crisis SWAT team, not your outside counsel.
"Will you help us prepare for investor due diligence?"
We protect your company so it can go public. We do not help you "raise rounds." If you are looking for a firm to make you attractive to VCs or to polish your pitch deck, we are not that firm. We make sure your S-1 does not get delayed by crisis, your banking partnerships survive regulatory scrutiny, and your narrative stays clean through the quiet period.
"How do I sign up? Is there a contract?"
Watchtower can be activated via consultation. Firefighter, Air Force One, and Siege Protocol require an application with BANT qualification. We review each application within 24 hours. If approved, we schedule a 30-minute crisis vulnerability assessment before onboarding. We only take clients we can genuinely help. Firefighter and above require a 90-day minimum — not because we want to lock you in, but because crisis response does not work as a month-to-month experiment, and your S-1 timeline does not pause for procurement cycles.
