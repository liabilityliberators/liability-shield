Using the Document Studio

This studio creates two documents: a client invoice and a structured Profit Leak Findings report. The preview updates as you type. Draft and saved-client data stay in this browser. Invoice delivery to Zoho happens only when you deliberately press the Zoho send button and use the secured connector.

1. Set up your business profile once

Open “Business profile” and enter your actual legal/business name, billing email and address. These details appear on the invoice and report footer. Do not use placeholder or unverified details on a client document.

2. Create an invoice

  • Enter the invoice number, dates, client billing details and any purchase-order reference.
  • Use line items to describe the agreed service clearly. The default Profit Leak Audit line is $2,000; confirm that amount against the engagement agreement.
  • Check tax treatment with a qualified tax professional where relevant. The calculator only applies the percentage you enter; it does not determine tax liability.
  • Verify payment method, network and address independently before issuing. The studio cannot validate a wallet address or confirm receipt of funds.

3. Send an invoice by email

  • Enter the recipient in the Client email field and confirm the invoice total, due date, payment network and wallet address.
  • Export the invoice as a PDF and save it to your computer.
  • For direct delivery, enter your secure Worker URL and private access key, connect Zoho once, then test the connection. The sending mailbox is fixed as support@liabilityliberators.com.
  • Click “Send invoice PDF via Zoho”. The studio creates a high-resolution invoice attachment, attaches it through the Zoho Mail API and sends it from your authenticated mailbox.
  • Use “Create Gmail draft” or “Open email app” only as alternative manual workflows. Use “Copy USDT payment address” to copy the wallet address; the email body also includes it as plain text.

4. Build the $2,000 audit report

  • Engagement brief: define the business question, period, entities/systems covered, what is included and what is excluded.
  • Data & evidence: list every data source, the period it covers, access received, population, sample size and coverage. Explain any missing or unreliable data.
  • Findings: create one entry for each distinct issue. State what the evidence shows, the calculation, assumptions, evidence reference, priority and status. Classify every one-time amount as recovery/credit, cost avoidance, potential exposure or not applicable. Do not double-count.
  • Roadmap: translate findings into practical actions, each linked to a finding, an owner, effort and target date.
  • Executive narrative: explain the commercial significance in plain English and identify decisions management needs to make.

5. What makes the audit worth the fee?

The value is not the number of pages. It is the defined review, evidence trail, transparent calculations, quantified and properly classified opportunities, management-ready priorities, and a practical implementation roadmap. Deliver only tests that were actually performed and evidence that was actually reviewed. The template cannot manufacture proof or guarantee a recovery.

6. Quality check before export

Use the report quality meter as a pre-flight reminder. Make sure every material finding has an evidence reference, calculation method, assumption, defensible status and a corresponding action. Reconcile figures to source records and check that two findings do not count the same transaction or contract value twice. Select “Final” only after your internal review.

7. Export, backup and saved clients

  • Export PDF: opens the browser's native print dialog. Choose Save as PDF, paper size A4, scale 100% or Fit to printable area, and enable Background graphics. This keeps text crisp and selectable instead of turning the page into a blurry screenshot. No pop-up or external PDF library is needed.
  • Download HTML: downloads the current document preview as a standalone HTML file. It is a readable copy, not a PDF.
  • Save client: saves invoice fields, report fields, records, findings and actions under the label you enter. Choose the saved label at the top of the panel to reload it.
Commercial boundary: this report is an operational and commercial review, not a statutory financial-statement audit, legal opinion, tax opinion or assurance engagement. Any implementation fees and any 10–25% performance component should be agreed separately in writing and calculated only on verified recovered value or savings, using a clear baseline and measurement period.
